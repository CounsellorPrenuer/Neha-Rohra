About Founder

Neha Rohra

SENIOR TALENT ACQUISITION & TALENT MANAGEMENT || COMPREHENSIVE HRBP ROLE || HR POLICIES AND AUDIT || HR LECTURER || CAREER COUNSELOR-PRENEUR
Neha Rohra brings 15 years of overall experience, with 12+ years in Human Resources, and a key focus on talent acquisition—particularly for premium roles. Her exposure to recruitment across industries and domains has given her a deep understanding of the skills, education pathways, and competencies required for different career goals. With experience supporting hiring decisions at both talent and organizational levels, she bridges what candidates bring with what roles truly need. Building on her HRBP work, Neha Rohra has also handled grievance management, supporting fair, empathetic resolution processes aligned with workplace well-being and business needs. Alongside her HR responsibilities, she has served an NGO, providing counselling to individuals in distress through a healing, growth-focused approach. She has also worked as a visiting lecturer across colleges for HR and contributed to IELTS English coaching. With a blend of hiring, counselling, and teaching—plus experience with top MNCs such as EY (EY Parthenon) and Capgemini—career advisory becomes her next logical step, while she gradually moves toward entrepreneurship with purpose and meaningful impact.
